# Carry Splits Privacy Policy

**Effective date: September 6, 2026**

Carry Splits is designed to keep shared-expense information on the user's device. This policy describes how Carry Splits handles information in version 1.0.

## Information We Collect

Carry Splits does not collect personal data from the app.

The app does not require or collect:

- names tied to an online account
- email addresses
- phone numbers
- contact lists
- precise or approximate location
- bank or payment account information
- advertising identifiers
- analytics identifiers
- browsing history
- search history
- health or fitness information

Participant names, expense descriptions, amounts, balances, and settlement records entered into Carry Splits are stored locally on the user's device for the app's functionality.

## Accounts

Carry Splits does not use accounts, profiles, sign-in, or cloud authentication.

## Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

Carry Splits does not include advertising, analytics SDKs, cross-app tracking, or behavioral profiling in version 1.0.

Carry Splits does not sell personal information.

## Local Storage

Carry Splits uses Apple's on-device data technologies to store app data locally. The developer does not operate a server that receives or synchronizes Carry Splits expense data.

Device backups are controlled by the user's Apple device and backup settings. Apple services and device backups are governed by Apple's own terms and privacy practices.

## Sharing

Carry Splits includes an optional Share action using the native iOS Share Sheet. Nothing is shared unless the user explicitly chooses to share a settlement summary.

If the user sends information to another app or service through the Share Sheet, the destination app or service handles that information under its own privacy policy.

## Data Deletion

Users can delete splits and related records from within Carry Splits. Removing the app from the device removes the app's active local data store from that device. Device backups, if any, are controlled by the user's Apple backup settings.

## Children

Carry Splits does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults because the app does not collect personal data from users.

## Security

Carry Splits relies on Apple's iOS application sandbox and platform security controls for local app data. No internet connection is required for the app's core functionality.

## Changes to This Policy

If Carry Splits changes its data practices, this policy will be updated and the App Store privacy disclosures will be revised as required.
