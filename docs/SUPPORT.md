# Carry Splits Support

Carry Splits is a local-first iPhone expense splitter designed to work without an account or internet connection.

## Common Questions

### Does Carry Splits require an account?

No. Carry Splits does not require sign-in, email, phone number, or a user profile.

### Does Carry Splits need internet access?

No. Core expense splitting, balances, settlement planning, persistence, and sharing preparation work offline.

### Where is my data stored?

Carry Splits stores app data locally on your iPhone using Apple's on-device data technologies.

### Can I recover a deleted split?

No. Deleting a split removes its active local record from Carry Splits. Device backups are controlled by your Apple backup settings.

### Why can’t I remove a participant?

Carry Splits prevents removal of a participant when that participant is referenced by existing expense or settlement history. This protects the accuracy of the ledger. You can rename the participant instead.

### Why can’t I save an exact split?

The exact amounts entered for all participants must add up to the total expense amount.

### Can I use multiple currencies in one split?

Not in version 1.0. Each split uses one currency to keep balances and settlements predictable.

### Does Carry Splits move money?

No. Carry Splits calculates and records who owes whom. It does not connect to banks or process payments.
